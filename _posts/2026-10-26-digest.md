Selon certaines informations, des pharmacies manquent de doses, mais la porte-parole Marianne Paquette affirme qu’il n’y a pas de pénurie dans la province. Le vaccin est gratuit pour certains groupes vulnérables; pour les autres, il coûte de 30 $ à 50 $.</p><p class="l-en summary" lang="en">About 750,000 Quebecers have booked vaccination appointments, nearly 140,000 more than at the same time last year, according to Santé Québec. Some pharmacies are reportedly short of doses, but spokesperson Marianne Paquette says there is no shortage in the province. The vaccine is free for some vulnerable groups; for others it costs $30 to $50.</p>
